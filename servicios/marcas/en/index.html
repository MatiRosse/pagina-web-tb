Argentina trademark protection

Why protection in Argentina requires a local filing strategy

Argentina is not listed among the current members of WIPO’s Madrid System. An international registration therefore cannot designate Argentina; protection is pursued through the Argentine national system.

We prepare and file your trademark application directly before INPI.
