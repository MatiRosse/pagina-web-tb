OUR EXPERIENCE

Tassara Law Firm & Bulgheroni

For more than 30 years, TB Abogados has provided comprehensive legal advice, combining extensive experience with close and personalized service. Founded as a family law firm, we now work with a multidisciplinary team of specialized attorneys and professionals, including accountants, a public translator and expert consultants. This approach allows us to handle labor, civil, commercial, probate and pension matters with a complete perspective. We meet clients in Buenos Aires and represent individuals and businesses throughout Argentina.
